How to connect

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. iPhone browsers can't reach printers, and neither can this page inside the Claude app. Open the downloaded file instead.
  2. Turn off Bluetooth on the PrepWizard tablet. The printer takes one connection at a time.
  3. Click Connect printer at the top and pick PrepWizardBix. After that, the app reconnects on its own when it opens. If it can't, one tap on Connect printer opens the list again.
  4. To make automatic reconnect work in Edge: go to edge://flags, search for “new permissions backend for Web Bluetooth”, set it to Enabled, and restart Edge. Connect once more after that. In Chrome, use chrome://flags instead.
  5. Blank label or nothing printed? Switch the printer language or the Bluetooth data channel above.
  6. When you're done, turn the tablet's Bluetooth back on so PrepWizard works again.

Google Sheet item list

Not set up

Keep your items in a Google Sheet. Each time the app opens, it checks the sheet and asks before changing your list.

  1. Make a sheet with these column headers in row 1: Item, Shelf life, and optionally Category.
  2. Shelf life is in days (3) or hours (4h). Category is anything you like, such as Prep, Received or Prod.
  3. Click Share and set General access to Anyone with the link as Viewer, then copy the link and paste it above.
  4. Labels made from the sheet show the item name, the prep time and the use-by date. You can still restyle any of them in Design. Your design is kept, and only its use-by changes if the sheet changes.
